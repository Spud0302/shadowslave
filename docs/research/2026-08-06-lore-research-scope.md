# Lore research comparison scope

**Research date:** 2026-08-06  
**Status:** working scope for the follow-up report; findings will be added separately after primary-text research

The follow-up lore report will compare current repository design and implementation against these systems:

1. Nightmare Spell infection and Carrier progression;
2. First Nightmare entry, role assignment, reconstructed history, conflict resolution, failure and appraisal;
3. Spell state, Soul Rank, Aspect Rank, Class and core count;
4. Aspects, Aspect Abilities, evolution and lineage interactions;
5. Flaws and their relationship to identity and power;
6. supernatural Attributes versus ordinary physical attributes;
7. Soul Essence, Soul Shards and advancement;
8. Dream Realm entry, Dream Anchors, Gateways and Awakening;
9. Memories, Echoes, Shadows and soul storage;
10. Gates, Nightmare Seeds, Nightmare Creatures and corruption;
11. True Names, Names, fate and appraisal boundaries;
12. multiplayer and multi-challenger Nightmare implications.

The report will use the project classifications `CANON`, `INFERRED`, `DESIGN` and `UNKNOWN`, cite chapter-level evidence where available, avoid copied chapter text, and distinguish current code from future architecture and optional modpack presentation.
