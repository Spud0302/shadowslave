# Section B — Ranks and progression

> **Status:** working verified pass. Major early-progression corrections are established; later natural progression is still being expanded.
>
> **Spoilers:** unrestricted.
>
> This section distinguishes three things that the project previously blurred together:
>
> 1. the **human/social title** (`Sleeper`, `Master`, `Saint`, etc.);
> 2. the **Rank/quality of the soul core** (`Dormant`, `Ascended`, etc.);
> 3. the **rank of the Aspect**, which is a separate variable and can be much higher or lower than the person's Soul Rank.

## B1. The full Rank ladder

**CONFIDENCE: CANON for the seven Soul Ranks; CANON/WIKI for human colloquial titles as noted**

The seven Soul Ranks, in ascending order, are:

1. **Dormant**
2. **Awakened**
3. **Ascended**
4. **Transcendent**
5. **Supreme**
6. **Sacred**
7. **Divine**

Sunny explicitly learns the seven-rank ladder during the First Nightmare material, and the Spell uses these same ranks for soul cores and Aspect ranks.

For humans, the commonly used titles are:

| Soul Rank | Human title | Confidence | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dormant | **Sleeper** | CANON | The Spell itself tends to call this state **Dreamer** in Sunny's runes; humans say Sleeper. |
| Awakened | **Awakened** | CANON | Confusingly, `Awakened` is also sometimes used as an umbrella term for all supernatural humans. |
| Ascended | **Master** | CANON | Reached through the Second Nightmare on the Spell-assisted path. |
| Transcendent | **Saint** | CANON | Reached through the Third Nightmare; gains a Transformation Ability. |
| Supreme | **Sovereign** | CANON | Associated with authority, Will and a Domain. |
| Sacred | **Spirit** | CANON/WIKI-backed by late chapters | A lesser deity produced by Apotheosis; a Spirit of a place, concept, law/source element, etc. |
| Divine | **God** | WIKI/current late-lore terminology | Current rank pages use `God (Divine)`. Direct late-primary wording should still be archived before treating this human colloquial title as exhaustively settled. |

`Mundane` is useful descriptive language for an ordinary human/soul, but the supernatural rank ladder begins at **Dormant**. Before Sunny completes his First Nightmare, his runes say:

- `Rank: Aspirant`

`Aspirant` is his Spell status as someone currently challenging the First Nightmare, not an eighth Soul Rank below Dormant.

## B2. What changes at each Rank?

**CONFIDENCE: CANON for Dormant through Transcendent; CANON/WIKI-late-lore for Supreme/Sacred; UNKNOWN in detail for Divine**

### Before completing the First Nightmare — Aspirant

Sunny's First-Nightmare status already contains a **Dormant Soul Core**, Attributes and a Dormant Aspect, but he is still called an **Aspirant** by the Spell.

Important distinction: an Aspirant is not yet a Sleeper/Dreamer. The First Nightmare appraisal has not occurred, the first Aspect Ability has not awakened, and the Flaw has not yet been bestowed/discovered through the Spell-assisted path.

Sources:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-2-slave-caravan.html
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Awakened

### Dormant — Dreamer / Sleeper — after the First Nightmare

This is the first major correction to the existing project documentation.

After Sunny survives the First Nightmare, the Spell addresses him as **Dreamer Sunless**. His status becomes:

- `Rank: Dreamer`
- `Shadow Core: Dormant`
- Aspect evolved to **[Shadow Slave]**
- first/Dormant Aspect Ability **[Shadow Control]** awakened
- Flaw received
- True Name awarded in his exceptional case

A normal Sleeper has the ability to perceive/interact with their Soul Core but **cannot yet consciously control Soul Essence**. They also have not established the normal recurring Dream Realm connection of an Awakened. At the winter solstice, they are forcibly transported to the Dream Realm for their first journey.

Sources:
- https://www.webnovel.com/book/22196546206090805/59929756203806543
- https://www.webnovel.com/book/22196546206090805/59953407766616922
- https://shadowslave.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Sleeper
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Sleeper

### Awakened — after returning from the Dream Realm for the first time

The **actual Awakening** occurs when a Sleeper successfully returns from their first Dream Realm journey.

Sunny's Chapter 354 transformation makes the distinction explicit:

- his core advances from Dormant to **Awakened**;
- his body is strengthened/reforged by the change;
- he gains the next Aspect Ability, **[Shadow Step]**;
- he becomes able to sense and consciously manipulate **Soul Essence**;
- an Awakened is connected/anchored to the Dream Realm and ordinarily enters it when sleeping.

This means `First Nightmare → Awakened` is **canonically wrong** for the normal Nightmare Spell path.

The correct sequence is:

```text
Carrier / Aspirant
  ↓ First Nightmare
Dreamer / Sleeper — Dormant
  ↓ first Dream Realm journey + successful return
Awakened — Awakened
```

Sources:
- https://www.webnovel.com/book/22196546206090805/64454794428866727 — Chapter 354, Awakening
- https://www.webnovel.com/book/22196546206090805/62497733776301988 — Nightmare Spell overview
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Nightmare_Spell_Path

### Ascended — Master — after the Second Nightmare

The Spell-assisted route is to conquer the **Second Nightmare**.

Ascension is more than a strength buff:

- soul/core quality becomes **Ascended**;
- a third Aspect Ability is awakened;
- body and spiritual body resonate/merge more completely;
- essence control becomes much finer and more efficient;
- a Master can travel into the Dream Realm **physically and voluntarily**, rather than being dragged there whenever they sleep;
- the body itself is profoundly perfected/strengthened.

Sunny's Ascension also demonstrates that existing Attributes and Aspect Abilities can **evolve during rank advancement**, not merely gain a new parallel power.

Sources:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-744-ascension.html
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Master

### Transcendent — Saint — after the Third Nightmare

The Spell-assisted route is to conquer the **Third Nightmare**.

The defining changes include:

- soul/core quality becomes **Transcendent**;
- a **Transformation Ability** is gained;
- the soul exceeds the old confines of the mortal body and becomes partially intertwined with the world/source element;
- Saints possess a pronounced supernatural **Presence**;
- their connection to the world lets them draw on ambient/spirit essence under appropriate conditions;
- their essence control and bodily reinforcement are vastly more sophisticated;
- they can act as small living bridges between worlds and transport limited living passengers;
- Saints can bind Citadels.

A Transformation Ability does **not** universally mean `turn into a giant animal`; forms and functions vary radically.

Sources:
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Saint
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Transformation_Ability

### Supreme — Sovereign — after the Fourth Nightmare or natural Supremacy

Supremacy is fundamentally about **authority**.

A Supreme:

- possesses Supreme-quality soul/essence;
- gains another very powerful Aspect Ability (often called the Supreme/Domain Ability in current documentation);
- wields a vastly stronger **Will** capable of being imposed on reality;
- develops/manifests a **Domain**, a sphere of authority tied to the person's Aspect/source and broader than a personal combat ability;
- becomes far stronger inside/supporting their Domain;
- can create/possess a **Dream Gate** bridging the waking world and Dream Realm;
- is increasingly rejected by Earth's mundane laws.

Important correction: **Domain and Supreme Aspect Ability are not simply the same thing.** Current lore treats the Domain as another natural function/expression of the Aspect/authority, even though the Supreme Ability may interact with it.

Natural Supremacy requires, at minimum, the development of overwhelming Will/authority and a nascent Domain; the Fourth Nightmare is the Spell's accelerated route.

Sources:
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Sovereign
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Domain

### Sacred — Spirit — Apotheosis / Fifth Nightmare

Sacred is a categorical change from **mortal to deity**, not just another larger stat multiplier.

Late lore describes Apotheosis as:

- becoming a **Spirit / lesser deity**;
- the soul becoming vast enough to encompass an entire realm and fuse with its governing laws/source element;
- a radical change in consciousness, perception and identity;
- requiring the being to become capable of bearing divinity, with loss/transformation of human nature as a real danger;
- producing Will qualitatively beyond Supreme Will;
- making Earth effectively inaccessible because the mundane realm rejects such a divine existence.

The **Fifth Nightmare** can force/accelerate Apotheosis. A natural route also exists, but late-story explanations emphasize that genuine Apotheosis is normally extremely slow and difficult.

Sources:
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Apotheosis
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Spirit

### Divine — God

**CONFIDENCE: CANON for the metaphysical definition; UNKNOWN for a complete human gameplay-like ability package**

Late explanations define a Divine being as a **true god** whose essence encompasses all existence and is woven into universal laws.

The Sixth Nightmare is associated with the final Divine step in Spell-era explanations, but the exact complete package of what a newly Divine human gains is not documented with the same practical specificity as Dormant through Supreme.

Do not invent `Divine Ability = X` from rank symmetry unless direct text establishes it.

Sources:
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Spirit
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Category:God

## B3. What is required to ascend between Ranks?

**CONFIDENCE: CANON, with two distinct paths**

There are two progression systems that reach the same metaphysical ladder:

1. **Nightmare Spell-assisted progression** — fast, structured, dangerous.
2. **Natural/ancient progression** — the underlying path that exists without the Spell.

### Nightmare Spell path

```text
Infected Carrier / Aspirant
  ↓ survive First Nightmare
Dormant Dreamer/Sleeper
  ↓ survive first Dream Realm journey and return through a Gateway
Awakened
  ↓ conquer Second Nightmare
Ascended / Master
  ↓ conquer Third Nightmare
Transcendent / Saint
  ↓ conquer Fourth Nightmare
Supreme / Sovereign
  ↓ conquer Fifth Nightmare
Sacred / Spirit
  ↓ conquer Sixth Nightmare
Divine / God
```

The First Nightmare is unusual: it does **not** promote a person all the way to Awakened Soul Rank. It creates the Sleeper/Dreamer stage and the first Aspect Ability/Flaw package.

### Natural path

The Spell did not create Ascension; it automates and accelerates it.

For early natural progression, late Rain material establishes that the order differs from Spell carriers:

- learn to **sense** soul essence;
- learn to **control/refine** it;
- form/reform a Soul Core through conscious cultivation;
- naturally **Awaken**;
- find/unseal Aspect and Flaw separately rather than having the Spell reveal them automatically.

For later ranks, natural advancement increasingly becomes conceptual/metaphysical:

- **Ascension:** refine essence/body until a qualitative change and reform the soul core;
- **Transcendence:** expand the soul beyond the body's mundane limits and establish a relationship with the world/source element;
- **Supremacy:** establish authority/Will and a Domain;
- **Apotheosis/Sacred:** become an embodiment/Spirit of the source element, crossing from mortal to divine.

The exact complete natural recipe is **not** a simple `consume N Soul Cores` rule.

Source:
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Paths_of_Ascension
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Sovereign
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Apotheosis
- Rain natural-Awakening chapters around 2029 onward.

### Soul shards are progression resources, not the universal rank-up button

For ordinary Spell carriers, absorbing Soul Shards increases the **capacity/power of the Soul Core** and essence reserves. It is not equivalent to automatically promoting Soul Rank from Awakened to Ascended.

The Spell's Nightmares perform/accelerate the qualitative Rank transition; natural cultivators must accomplish the corresponding refinement themselves.

Sunny is an exceptional progression case because his Shadow Core uses **Shadow Fragments** and he can form multiple cores/classes. Do not generalize his core-count mechanic to ordinary humans.

Sources:
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Spell_Status_Page
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Paths_of_Ascension

## B4. How do the Attributes on the Spell Status Page work?

**CONFIDENCE: CANON — and the premise in the original research brief is wrong**

There is **no canonical universal RPG-stat block of `Vitality`, `Endurance`, Strength, Dexterity, etc.** on the Shadow Slave Spell Status Page.

In the novel, **Attributes are named passive traits/affinities**.

Sunny's early runes, for example, contain:

- **[Fated]**
- **[Mark of Divinity]**
- **[Child of Shadows]**

The novel explains that Attributes represent natural traits and affinities and can provide passive effects. They can be:

- innate;
- inherited or acquired through a First Nightmare role;
- granted/evolved by lineages;
- evolved during advancement;
- gained from unusual supernatural events.

Examples across the setting include **[Blood Weave]**, **[Bone Weave]**, **[Dreamspawn]**, **[Nephilim]**, **[Poet]**, **[Godheart]**, **[Battle Master]**, etc.

Therefore questions such as `what is a typical Vitality score for an Awakened?` have the answer:

> **UNKNOWN / invalid premise — Shadow Slave does not use that canonical stat field.**

Physical power, endurance, speed and resilience absolutely increase with Rank, essence reinforcement, Attributes, Aspect powers, Class/core quantity in exceptional beings, training, body transformations and lineage changes — but they are not presented as a standard numeric STR/VIT/END character sheet.

- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-2-slave-caravan.html

## B5. What is `Divine Sight`, and what can one Awakened read about another?

**CONFIDENCE: UNKNOWN for `Divine Sight` because no general canon mechanic by that name was found; CANON for the actual information-reading powers below**

The original research question appears to contain another false premise: **no general Shadow Slave ability/mechanic called `Divine Sight` has been verified** as something every Awakened possesses.

Ordinary Awakened do **not** simply inspect another player's full Spell status page as a baseline feature.

### Cassie's information-reading ability is Aspect-specific

Cassie's Dormant Aspect Ability is fundamentally an **insight/revelation** power. The Spell translates that supernatural insight into familiar runes.

Its information access deepens as she advances:

- as a **Sleeper/Dormant**, she can read **Attributes** of nearby beings and information about Nightmare Creatures;
- after **Awakening**, she can additionally perceive **Aspect names and ranks**;
- after **Ascension**, her first Ability improves further so she can perceive **Aspect Abilities**;
- later advancement broadens/deepens the information she can retrieve.

Her ability notably does **not** make another person's Flaw freely visible as a standard low-rank inspection field.

Sources:
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Cassie/Runes
- Rain/Cassie discussion around Chapters 2029–2040.

### Sunny later gains a separate Domain/Spell interaction

Very late material gives Supreme Sunny access to the runes of members of the Shadow Clan/subjects tied into his Domain. This should **not** be generalized backward into a universal Awakened ability without evidence. It may be specific to his Aspect/Domain/Spell relationship.

### Conclusion

The safe current answer is:

> **There is no verified universal `Divine Sight` system where any Awakened can inspect another Awakened. Information-reading is power-specific. Cassie is the canonical major example, and her readable fields expand with Rank.**

If `Divine Sight` exists somewhere as an exact term in an older note, it should be treated as project terminology until a direct canon occurrence is located.

# Major corrections to the existing project lore/reference

1. **First Nightmare does not make a normal carrier Awakened.** It makes them a Dormant **Dreamer/Sleeper**.
2. **Awakening happens after the first successful Dream Realm journey/return**, such as Sunny escaping the Forgotten Shore.
3. **The First Nightmare grants/awakens the first (Dormant) Aspect Ability and Flaw**, not the Awakened Ability.
4. **Attributes are not Vitality/Endurance-style stats.** They are named passive supernatural traits/affinities.
5. **`Divine Sight` is not a verified universal mechanic.** Cassie's progressive rune-reading is Aspect-specific.
6. **Domain is not simply synonymous with the Supreme Aspect Ability.** They are related but distinct expressions of Supremacy.
7. **The old note's `Divine human title = unnamed` is probably stale.** Current late-lore pages use `God`, but direct primary wording is still being archived.

# Design consequences — proposals, not canon

The project owner has explicitly allowed design/implementation suggestions on this branch.

## 1. Split Phase 1's current end state

The existing state machine should eventually distinguish:

```text
Untouched / mundane human
→ Carrier / Aspirant
→ First Nightmare
→ Dreamer / Sleeper (Dormant)
→ first Dream Realm expedition
→ Awakening (Awakened)
```

That change creates a valuable gameplay stage instead of merely adding lore correctness: **Sleepers already have an Aspect, Flaw and first Ability but still lack conscious essence control and are waiting for the compulsory first Dream Realm journey.**

## 2. Treat Winter Solstice / first Dream Realm journey as a progression event, not just another Nightmare

The first journey is structurally different from a numbered Nightmare. It is a long survival/exploration arc in the actual Dream Realm whose win condition is fundamentally **find/reach a Gateway and return**.

That naturally supports a much more open-ended progression experience than `survive timer → kill boss`.

## 3. Replace generic RPG attributes with named Traits

If the current `/trigger soul` screen has generic attributes inspired by RPG stats, those should be separated from canon **Attributes**.

Two clean options later:

- keep hidden/internal Minecraft combat stats but never call them Spell Attributes; or
- expose only named Shadow-Slave-style Attributes and let vanilla/Minecraft numbers remain implementation details.

This prevents terms like `Vitality` from becoming fake lore through UI repetition.

## 4. Ability slots should track rank milestones

A generic player progression model can mirror the actual ladder:

```text
Sleeper       → Dormant Ability
Awakened      → Awakened Ability
Master        → Ascended Ability
Saint         → Transformation Ability
Sovereign     → Supreme Ability + Domain development
Spirit        → Apotheosis-scale change
```

Existing abilities should also be allowed to evolve on rank-up; Sunny proves advancement is not merely `append one new spell`.

## 5. Information inspection should be earned/specialized

Do not make `look at player → read full Aspect/Flaw/status` a baseline multiplayer feature if canon fidelity matters.

Instead, status-reading can be:

- private by default;
- selectively shared;
- exposed by information/revelation Aspects;
- expanded by rank/Domain-specific powers.

That creates room for secrecy around Flaws, which the novel repeatedly treats as strategically important information.

# Open questions

2. Exact ability package of a newly Divine human; current lore is much more metaphysical than mechanical here.
3. Full natural method for each rank between Ascended and Sacred — late lore provides principles but not always procedural steps.
4. Exact general rules for Dream Gates and whether every naturally Supreme human would express them identically.
5. Whether Sunny's late ability to inspect Domain-subject runes is unique to his Shadow Domain/Mark of Shadows or a general Sovereign+Spell feature.
6. Precise quantitative physical/lifespan scaling by Rank. The story gives qualitative and example-based differences rather than a universal table of numbers.

# Things I did not ask about that matter

1. **`Rank` and `Soul Core` can be displayed as separate fields.** Sunny is `Rank: Dreamer` while his core is `Dormant`, which is exactly why using one integer for both social progression and core quality can become confusing.
2. **Aspect Rank is independent of Soul Rank.** A Dormant Sleeper can possess a Divine Aspect.
3. **Class/core count is a separate axis again.** Sunny and other Divine Aspect holders are exceptional humans capable of multiple cores/classes; normal humans have one core.
4. **The Spell-assisted path reverses/automates parts of natural cultivation.** Rain is crucial evidence that the familiar player-facing order is not the underlying metaphysical order.
5. **The term `Awakened` is overloaded in canon.** It can mean the specific Awakened Soul Rank/human stage or supernatural humans collectively. Documentation should say which one it means.
