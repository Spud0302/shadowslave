# Section F — Vocabulary, institutions, Spell voice and world texture

> **Status:** verified working pass, with current political-state caveats.
>
> **Spoilers:** unrestricted.
>
> This section is less about mechanical rules and more about using the setting's own language. One important result is that the Dream Realm does **not** have one universal visual palette; its consistency comes from tone, scale and metaphysical motifs rather than one color/material set.

## F1. What vocabulary does the world use?

**CONFIDENCE: CANON unless marked otherwise**

### Human progression terms

| Term | Meaning / use |
| --- | --- |
| **Carrier** | A human infected by the Nightmare Spell before completing the First Nightmare. The Seed grows inside their soul and exhaustion builds toward the trial. |
| **Aspirant** | The Spell's formal status/title for someone undertaking the First Nightmare. Sunny's runes use `Rank: Aspirant` during the trial. |
| **Dreamer** | The Spell's formal title after surviving the First Nightmare. Sunny is addressed as `Dreamer Sunless`. |
| **Sleeper** | The ordinary human/social term for a Dormant Dreamer awaiting or surviving the first Dream Realm journey. |
| **Awakened** | Both the specific post-Sleeper Rank/title **and** an overloaded umbrella term for supernatural humans in ordinary speech. Context matters. |
| **Master** | Human title for an Ascended. |
| **Saint** | Human title for a Transcendent. |
| **Sovereign** | Human title for a Supreme. |
| **Spirit** | Late-era human/metaphysical term for a Sacred being / lesser deity. |
| **God** | Divine being / true deity. Current late-lore terminology uses this naturally, though the early human world had no examples. |
| **Legacy** | Broad adjective/noun for inherited Awakened families, traditions and institutions. Do not confuse **Legacy clan**, **Aspect Legacy**, and **Legacy Relic**. |
| **True Name** | A supernatural name granted/earned by exceptional people; not simply a nickname or title. |

Sources:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-2-slave-caravan.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-15.html
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Kai
- `section-b-ranks-and-progression.md`

### Nightmare and Dream Realm terms

| Term | Meaning / use |
| --- | --- |
| **Nightmare** | A Spell-mediated historical trial built around a central conflict. |
| **First Nightmare** | The individually tailored first trial whose Seed grows inside a Carrier. |
| **Seed of Nightmare / Nightmare Seed** | A later Seed growing in the Dream Realm that can be challenged and can bloom into a Gate. |
| **Call of Nightmare / the Call** | Compulsion/attraction emitted by a Seed, drawing Spell carriers toward the challenge. |
| **Nightmare Gate / Gate** | Hostile breach from the Dream Realm into the waking world. |
| **Dream Gate** | Sovereign-created passage in the opposite useful direction; not the same as a Nightmare Gate. |
| **Gateway** | Stable Dream Realm location used by lower-rank Awakened to return/anchor; associated with Citadels. |
| **Citadel** | Major stronghold/anchor site in the Dream Realm, frequently containing a Gateway and possessing unusual ancient/metaphysical functions. |
| **Dream Realm** | The vast world into which Sleepers/Awakened travel; later understood as a realm formed through the consumption/merging of other worlds. |
| **Dreamscape** | Artificial/virtual Awakened network used for communication, entertainment and combat; **not** another name for the Dream Realm. |
| **Nightmare Creature** | Modern standard term for corrupted/profane creatures threatening humanity. |
| **Abomination** | Common informal/general term for Nightmare Creatures and corrupted beings. |
| **Corrupted** | Ancient/metaphysical term for beings transformed by Corruption; also the name of one specific Nightmare Creature Rank, so context matters. |
| **Defiled** | Especially associated with humans/beings corrupted in the Tomb/Third-Nightmare context; not a synonym that should replace every use of Nightmare Creature. |

Early historical memories show terminology evolving. In the first chaotic period, survivors spoke of the flood of the **infected**; later those creatures became standardized as **Nightmare Creatures**. Ancient people often conceptualized them through **Corruption** rather than the modern taxonomy.

Sources:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-1930-bright-future.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-2749-third-times-the-charm.html
- `section-d-nightmares.md`

### Soul / power-system terms

Terms that should retain their canon meaning:

- **Soul Sea** — inner landscape of the soul.
- **Soul Core** — essence nexus/fulcrum inside the soul.
- **Soul Essence** — spiritual energy/reserve.
- **Soul Shard** — crystallized remnant/essence of a broken conventional core.
- **Aspect** — supernatural nature/specialization belonging to the person.
- **Aspect Ability** — expression of that Aspect unlocked/deepened through Rank.
- **Innate Ability** — separate Aspect-linked condition/power, not simply the Dormant Ability.
- **Flaw** — law-of-imperfection expression tied to the individual; not a generic debuff.
- **Attribute** — named passive supernatural trait/affinity, **not** STR/DEX/Vitality stats.
- **Memory** — soul-stored Woven artifact.
- **Echo** — soulless magical replica.
- **Shadow** — context-sensitive ordinary word, but capitalized as Sunny's Aspect-specific creature category when relevant.
- **Domain** — a Supreme's authority made manifest; not just an area-of-effect spell.
- **Will** — metaphysical authority/force increasingly central at Supreme and above.

## F2. Factions, cities and institutions relevant to an Awakened's life

**CONFIDENCE: CANON for the institutions; current political control is time-sensitive and noted separately**

The setting changes dramatically over the course of the novel. A mod should distinguish **institutional vocabulary that persists** from **who currently owns it**.

### Government / United Human Government

The **Government** is the central civic and military institution of the waking world.

Its practical relationship with Awakened includes:

- tracking registered Awakened;
- responding to Nightmare Gates;
- military service and irregular Awakened units;
- issuing special communicators / duty expectations;
- maintaining citizenship and administrative systems;
- offering **contribution points** that can be exchanged for rare resources/credits;
- running the Awakened Academy;
- later administering major territory and cities in the Dream Realm.

Historically, the **United Human Government** emerged as disparate post-Spell strongholds were consolidated under a shared administration.

Sources:
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Government
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-1930-bright-future.html

### Current-state contradiction to keep visible

The current Government wiki header still lists **Jet** as leader, but Chapter **3012** has Jet explicitly **resign/retire from government service** after spending a year training replacements, partly because her personal influence had become too dominant for an institution she believed should not be ruled by one Awakened.

Therefore:

- **CANON:** Jet was the de facto leader / dominant champion of the Government after the Throne War.
- **CANON:** Jet resigns in Chapter 3012 before challenging the Fourth Nightmare.
- **UNKNOWN from the checked material:** the exact named individual, if any, who formally leads the Government after her resignation.

This is a concrete example of a wiki infobox lagging late novel state.

Sources:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-3012-free-agent.html
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Government

### Awakened Academy

The **Awakened Academy** is a government-run compound in the **Northern Quadrant Siege Capital**.

Its core role is preparing willing **Sleepers** for their first journey into the Dream Realm. Training includes combat, survival, Dream Realm knowledge and the practical culture of Awakened life.

It is a major social funnel: people from different Quadrants and backgrounds meet peers here before the winter solstice.

Source:
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Awakened_Academy

### Northern Quadrant Siege Capital — NQSC / “the City”

The **Northern Quadrant Siege Capital (NQSC)** is the largest major surviving Earth megacity and for much of the story functions as the industrial, military and administrative heart of humanity.

Its visual/social identity is deliberately unlike medieval fantasy:

- vertical megacity architecture;
- reinforced alloys and graphene composites;
- aerial bridges and tunnels;
- armored transport;
- fortified ports;
- spelltech integrated with modern/post-apocalyptic technology;
- sharp social inequality between secure citizenship and the outskirts.

Source:
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Northern_Quadrant_Siege_Capital

### Legacy Clans and Great Clans

A **Legacy Clan** is an Awakened family/institution built around inherited supernatural power, accumulated knowledge, relics, training and influence.

Historically dominant Great Clans include:

- **Valor** — tied to Bastion, martial/forging tradition, formerly ruled by Anvil.
- **Song** — tied to Ravenheart, formerly ruled by Ki Song.
- **House of Night** — maritime Stormsea power centered on Night Garden; originally an alliance rather than one blood family.
- **Immortal Flame** — Nephis's ancestral clan, later restored and politically dominant after the Throne War.

Late politics make these labels historical as well as current: Valor is largely dismantled, Song fragments into the domains/clans of Ki Song's daughters, House of Night is disbanded/absorbed, and Immortal Flame becomes the center of the Human Domain.

Sources:
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Legacy_Clans
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/House_of_Night
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Immortal_Flame_Clan/History
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Valor_Clan/History
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Song_Clan/History

### Human Domain / Longing Domain

After the Throne War, Nephis establishes a unified **Human Domain**, later distinguished metaphysically as her **Longing Domain**.

This is more than a political nation: at Supreme Rank, `Domain` has a supernatural meaning tied to authority and the Sovereign's Aspect/source. Political territory and metaphysical Domain overlap but are not identical concepts.

Source:
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Human_Domain

### Bastion

**Bastion** is one of humanity's Great Citadels and for much of the novel the seat of Valor.

Its identity deliberately blends beauty and concealed horror:

- vast castle/city;
- tranquil Mirror Lake;
- fairy-tale appearance;
- false/true versions connected to the Demon of Imagination;
- surrounding ash left by the destroyed living forest/Titan;
- entrenched human settlement and military infrastructure.

After the Throne War it is administered by **Effie as Steward of the East**; later events may further alter active leadership.

Source:
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Bastion

### Ravenheart / Jade Palace

**Ravenheart** is the great Citadel historically controlled by Song.

Its defining image is severe beauty:

- ancient palace between snowy mountains and volcanoes;
- once white jade, blackened over ages into obsidian-like glass;
- enormous chasm and titanic stone bridge;
- a major human city, not merely one clan castle.

After the Throne War Kai is named **Steward of the West**, while the former Song princesses retain major power throughout the western territories. Later Kai prepares to leave for the Fourth Nightmare, so again the political title should not be frozen forever.

Sources:
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Ravenheart_%28Citadel%29
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Kai

### Night Garden / Stormsea

**Night Garden** is a Great Citadel shaped like a colossal living/wandering ship-city, originally associated with the Demon of Repose and later House of Night.

It travels the violent **Stormsea**, survives colossal waves and lightning, and can consume attacking abominations into its living structure.

After House of Night's fall it becomes a major Government stronghold and refugee city; the post-war Government also begins establishing formal Dream Realm schools there.

Source:
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Night_Garden

### Citadel / Gateway / anchor as everyday geography

For an Awakened, location is often expressed not as ordinary country/state geography but by:

- which **Citadel** they are anchored to;
- which **Gateway** they can use;
- which region of the Dream Realm surrounds it;
- which clan/domain controls the local safe territory;
- whether a Saint/Sovereign can physically transport them.

These are basic social/geographical terms, not only endgame lore.

## F3. What is the tone of the Nightmare Spell's messages?

**CONFIDENCE: CANON**

The Spell has a very recognizable voice. It should not be written like a chatty quest NPC.

### Surface style

Most ordinary messages are:

- **bracketed**;
- concise;
- declarative;
- formal;
- emotionally restrained;
- often built from repeated templates.

Examples of message classes include:

```text
[Prepare for appraisal…]
[Your nightmare is over.]
[You have slain …]
[You have received a Memory …]
[The First Seal is broken.]
[Awakening dormant powers…]
```

Source:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-15.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-743-appraisal.html

### Appraisals become mythic narration

When the Spell summarizes a Nightmare, the register changes.

It recounts the challenger as an archetypal figure rather than using mundane personal detail — e.g. a shadow, slave, hero, monster, prophet — and compresses months of events into elevated mythic narration.

The narration emphasizes:

- roles;
- transformations;
- betrayals/choices;
- major kills;
- resolution of the central conflict;
- relationship to fate.

This makes appraisal text feel like a **legend being written**, not a scoreboard recap.

Source:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-743-appraisal.html

### The Spell has dry irony / apparent personality

The Spell is not emotionally neutral in every line.

Sunny's First Nightmare ends with the appraisal **glorious**, followed by a dry jab about his treachery. The text repeatedly leaves open whether this is actual sentience, the personality of Weaver encoded in the sorcery, or merely how the system expresses itself.

The safest characterization is:

> **formal cosmic system with flashes of cruel, dry wit**

not:

> friendly AI assistant

Source:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-15.html

### Its “voice” can feel cosmic rather than audible

In ordinary waking interaction, the Spell can whisper in a familiar/subtle way. In the post-Nightmare void, Sunny describes its voice as if **the universe itself were speaking**.

This gives the same interface two scales:

- intimate whisper / rune notification;
- overwhelming metaphysical pronouncement.

Source:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-15.html

### Runes communicate meaning, not necessarily literal English writing

Later lore repeatedly shows that the Spell's runes are an interface/translation of deeper supernatural information. Cassie's insight can be rendered as the same kind of readable runes; Aspect names may admit multiple English renderings.

Therefore the canonical requirement is **meaning and tone**, not preserving a rigid English syntax as though Weaver authored a localization file.

### Design consequence — proposal

Spell UI text should probably have at least three registers:

1. **notification** — terse bracketed messages;
2. **status/runes** — structured supernatural readout;
3. **appraisal** — short mythic retelling with occasional cutting irony.

That would feel much closer to the novel than using the same Minecraft chat style for everything.

## F4. Recurring imagery, materials and phenomena

**CONFIDENCE: CANON for individual motifs; INFERRED for the overall aesthetic synthesis**

### Nightmare Spell — the strongest consistent visual identity

The Spell has an unusually stable visual vocabulary across the novel:

- **endless black void** between dream and reality;
- **myriad stars / souls**;
- **silver strings of light**;
- enormous **web/tapestry/net** structures;
- **nexuses and constellations**;
- silver/shimmering **runes**;
- the **Strings of Fate** as the material from which its universal weave is formed.

Late visions confirm this is not merely Sunny's metaphor: the Spell itself was Woven through the Strings of Fate and anchored into a cosmic network of souls.

Sources:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-15.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-743-appraisal.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-2412-weavers-lullaby.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-2832-those-forgotten.html

### Soul imagery

The recurring interior vocabulary is similarly astronomical/oceanic:

- **Soul Sea**;
- Soul Core as a **sun/star** hanging above it;
- Memories/Echoes as lights/spheres orbiting the core;
- multiple cores becoming multiple suns;
- preserved shadows/entities occupying the inner landscape.

The setting repeatedly mixes **cosmic scale** with an inward personal space.

Sources:
- `section-e-soul-cores-corruption-echoes.md`
- `section-c-memories.md`

### Dream Realm — no universal palette

**INFERRED conclusion:** the Dream Realm should not be reduced to `dark purple nightmare biome`.

Its regions are radically different:

- Forgotten Shore — black water, coral, crimson labyrinth, enormous ruined structures, artificial sun/Crimson Spire.
- Chained Isles — islands suspended in an immense sky, chains, white marble, crushing depth below.
- Antarctica / waking-world breaches — brutal military/post-apocalyptic ruin rather than fantasy gothic.
- Godgrave — colossal divine skeleton/bone landscape, scarlet jungle, ash, hollows, lethal sunlight.
- Ravenheart — white snow, blackened jade/obsidian palace, volcanic fire.
- Bastion — mirror lake, castle, beautiful illusion, hidden broken reality, ash of a slain living forest.
- Stormsea — darkness, violent ocean, lightning, living ship-citadel.
- Tomb of Ariel / Great River — impossible enclosed world, flowing time, mist, colossal river and ancient structures.

What unifies them is not color. It is:

- **impossible scale**;
- ancient layers of civilization;
- beautiful scenes hiding lethal rules;
- ruins whose purpose exceeds human understanding;
- landscapes that are themselves supernatural systems;
- repeated evidence that the world has been wounded by divine history.

Sources:
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Bastion
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Ravenheart_%28Citadel%29
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Night_Garden
- regional material throughout the novel

### Aspect imagery is individual, not standardized

There is no universal `Aspect aura` color.

Examples deliberately contrast:

- **Sunny / Shadow Slave:** darkness, shadow, black/onyx, lightless/transparent Shadow Cores, silhouettes, flowing shadow.
- **Nephis / Light Bringer:** white flame, radiance, warmth, incandescent light, later metaphysical longing.
- **Mordret:** mirrors, reflections, polished/silver surfaces, divided selves.
- **Cassie:** perception, eyes/sight/blindness, memory, revelation rather than one elemental palette.

Therefore procedurally generated Aspects should not all inherit one Spell visual language. **The Spell UI can be silver; the Aspect itself should establish its own motif.**

### Material vocabulary worth preserving

Frequently resonant materials/images include:

- **obsidian / black glass / black stone**;
- **white marble / ivory / bone**;
- **jade**;
- **silver** for runes, reflections, weave and enchanted surfaces;
- **gold / golden ichor** for divinity;
- **ash** as the remainder of destroyed civilizations/creatures;
- **blood / crimson / scarlet** for violent living corruption in some regions;
- **mirrors** and reflective water as metaphysical boundaries;
- **chains** as both literal ancient architecture and recurring imagery of slavery, fate and restraint.

These are recurring motifs, **not a rule that every Shadow Slave structure must contain them**.

### Tone synthesis

**INFERRED:** the strongest visual design principle is:

> **sublime first, horrifying second.**

The novel often presents something gorgeous, monumental or serene, then reveals that its beauty is produced by a corpse, a curse, a daemon's illusion, a dead civilization or a predatory metaphysical law.

That contrast is more faithful to Shadow Slave than making every environment uniformly grim.

# Corrections / terminology hazards

1. **Do not call a Sleeper an Awakened in the specific Rank sense.** The broad social use of `Awakened` is overloaded and can conceal the progression distinction.
2. **Do not call Dreamscape the Dream Realm.** Dreamscape is an artificial network.
3. **Do not use `Gate` for every portal without a qualifier.** Nightmare Gate, Dream Gate and Gateway have different functions.
4. **Do not call all magic gear `soulbound`.** See Section C.
5. **Do not call every corrupted entity `Corrupted`.** `Corrupted` is also a specific creature Rank.
6. **Do not freeze political control to one novel era.** Valor/Song/House of Night/Government leadership changes materially over time.
7. **The current Government wiki is internally stale about Jet.** It lists her as leader while also citing her Chapter 3012 resignation.
8. **Do not give every Dream Realm biome the Spell's silver/black aesthetic.** The silver fate-weave belongs most strongly to the Spell itself.

# Design consequences — proposals, not canon

## 1. Establish a project lexicon and forbid overloaded substitutes in UI

Use exact terms for exact states:

```text
Carrier → Aspirant → Dreamer/Sleeper → Awakened → Master → Saint → Sovereign → Spirit → God
```

Keep `Soul Rank`, `Aspect Rank`, `Class`, `Memory Rank`, and `Memory Tier` separately named everywhere in code/UI.

## 2. Give the Spell its own UI grammar

A visual/text layer can consistently use:

- dark/black backdrop;
- silver runes;
- subtle star/nexus motifs;
- bracketed notifications;
- mythic appraisal prose;
- sparse sound design/whisper rather than conventional RPG fanfare.

Then let individual Aspects break away into their own visual identities.

## 3. Build regional art direction instead of one “Dream Realm dimension” theme

The Dream Realm should feel like a collection of dangerous worlds/ecosystems connected by one metaphysics.

Each major generated/hand-authored region can define:

```text
sky_rule
terrain_materials
ambient_hazard
ancient_civilization_layer
signature_abominations
impossible_landmark
beauty/horror contrast
```

That preserves setting texture much better than one permanent dark fog shader.

## 4. Make institutions matter to the player loop

Rather than using clans only as lore names, their canonical functions suggest distinct social systems:

- Government — Gate response, duty, contribution points, public service.
- Academy — onboarding/training before first Dream Realm journey.
- Legacy clans — inherited Attributes/lineages, resources, training, political obligations.
- Citadel authorities — safe territory, Gateway access, markets, missions, local law.

These are implementation proposals; the exact mechanics are not canon.

## 5. Keep political state data-driven

Because the setting's control map changes repeatedly, do not hard-code `Bastion = Valor forever` into world logic.

Represent:

```text
citadel
current_controller
historical_controller
steward/ruler
Domain affiliation
Gateway state
```

That leaves room for multiplayer conquest and later story-era configurations without renaming the location itself.

# Things I did not ask about that matter

1. **The setting is not medieval fantasy.** The waking world is a technologically advanced, ecologically damaged megacity civilization using spelltech, synthetic food, modern bureaucracy, social media and military industry alongside magic.
2. **Political names and metaphysical names overlap.** `Domain`, `Clan`, `Citadel`, `Gate` and even `Awakened` can mean different layers depending on context; documentation must be precise.
3. **The Spell is itself a piece of sorcery, not the source of all magic.** Its visual language should therefore feel like a specific ancient system imposed on deeper metaphysics, not like the universal aesthetic of every supernatural phenomenon.
4. **Information has texture in this world.** Runes, dossiers, True Names, hidden Flaws, secret Aspect ranks, historical records and forbidden knowledge all carry real strategic/metaphysical weight.
5. **Beauty is repeatedly weaponized by the setting.** Safe-looking lakes, castles, forests, stars and dreams can conceal lethal laws; environments should not telegraph danger only by looking ugly.
