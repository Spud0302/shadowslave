characters' Aspects without ever revealing the formal name shown in the runes. Therefore this section

### Contradiction / bad label: Jet and “Soul Reaper”

**CONFIDENCE: CANON**

Do **not** treat **Soul Reaper** as Jet's formal Aspect name. The novel uses *Soul Reaper* as her
nickname/title, derived from the terrifying fact that her Awakened Ability attacks the soul directly.
Current runes material gives an Aspect rank and abilities without a formal Aspect name.

This is an example of why old wiki labels, epithets and character titles must not be automatically
copied into an Aspect-name list.

## A2. Naming patterns

**CONFIDENCE: INFERRED**

The safely verified corpus is too small to infer a universal naming grammar.

Verified names:

- **Temple Slave**
- **Shadow Slave**
- **Light Bringer**

All three happen to be two-part noun/title constructions. That is **not enough evidence** to conclude
that Aspects must be two words, that single-word names do not exist, or that an `X of Y` form is
impossible.

There is also a translation problem: **Light Bringer** can be interpreted/rendered as **Fire Bearer**.
The runes communicate meaning rather than ordinary written English, so fine-grained English naming
rules are less canonical than they first appear.

The stronger pattern is semantic rather than grammatical:

- **Shadow Slave** describes a broad metaphysical identity — a divine shadow longing for a master —
  from which many different powers and a dangerous condition emerge.
- **Light Bringer** is broader than “fire user”; its description contains radiance, warmth and longing,
  and longing later becomes fundamental to Nephis's Domain path.
- **Temple Slave** describes the Nightmare identity/role from which Sunny initially inherited the
  Aspect.

**INFERENCE:** an Aspect name appears more likely to express a person's supernatural
**nature/archetype** than to be a literal label for one combat ability. This is supported by the three
names above, but the sample is too small to call it a canon naming rule.

Sources:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-2-slave-caravan.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-15.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-1678-path-to-supremacy.html
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Aspect

Notes: canon currently leaves substantial freedom in the *grammar* of unknown Aspect names because it
simply has not supplied a large enough named sample to reverse-engineer one safely.

## A3. How is an Aspect determined?

**CONFIDENCE: CANON for the principles; UNKNOWN for the exact mechanism**

### The Nightmare Spell does not create the underlying Aspect

Rain establishes this decisively. She Awakens naturally without carrying the Spell. Chapter 2029
explains that the original/natural Awakened had to **find and unseal their Aspect themselves**, and many
never did. The Spell is therefore not the source of the Aspect; it is an interface/accelerator that can
help reveal and unseal something belonging to the person.

The community Aspect definition describes an Aspect as a confluence of one's **innate affinities and
choices**, manifested as a magical specialization, and states that it belongs to the person rather than
to the Spell. That wording is consistent with the natural-Awakening material.

### A First Nightmare still matters

Sunny complicates the picture because his First Nightmare role contributed real, persistent elements.
Chapter 2029 explicitly says that **[Temple Slave]**, **[Child of Shadows]** and **[Mark of Divinity]**
came from the nameless temple slave whose body he inhabited, whereas **[Fated]** was innately Sunny's.

The role itself is also stated not to be random. Chapter 218 says it was common knowledge that the
Spell did not choose an Aspirant's role randomly. Sunny sees a deep similarity of **fate** between
himself and the temple slave — similar birth circumstances, social position, loss of family and so on —
but also says the actual principle governing role/event selection remained largely unclear.

Then Sunny radically changes the expected course of events, receives Shadow God's blessing, earns a
Glorious appraisal and is offered an evolution of **Temple Slave → Shadow Slave**.

Therefore the evidence supports several interacting influences:

1. **the person** — affinities, nature, choices;
2. **the Nightmare role/history** assigned to a Spell carrier;
3. **what happens in the trial**, which can at least in exceptional cases change/evolve the Aspect.

### Rain strengthens the “choices/nature” side without giving an algorithm

Rain first naturally Awakens without an Aspect. Much later her Aspect begins to stir amid war and her
desire to save lives, but is finally unsealed after helping complete a road across the Collarbone Plain.
The story explicitly frames that as fitting: her life had been shaped by war, but ultimately more by
**creation than destruction**.

That is strong evidence that the way a person actually lives and chooses can matter to Aspect
realization. It still does **not** provide a deterministic formula.

### What canon does *not* establish

**UNKNOWN:** no source found gives a weighting or algorithm such as:

- X% personality;
- Y% First-Nightmare performance;
- creature killed determines theme;
- random roll from a table.

No evidence found supports a general `kill creature X → receive an Aspect based on X` rule.

Sources:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-15.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-218-shadow-dance.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-2029-fortune-telling.html

Notes: canon gives **constraints and examples, not a generation algorithm**. That gap should remain

## A4. Can an Aspect change, grow or gain new expressions? Relationship to Ability, Legacy and Relic

**CONFIDENCE: CANON**

Yes, in several distinct ways.

### 1. The rank of the Aspect itself can evolve

Sunny's First Nightmare is the clearest example:

- Dormant **[Temple Slave]**
- evolves after appraisal
- Divine **[Shadow Slave]**

This is not merely a soul-rank increase; the Spell explicitly calls it the evolution of the Aspect.

Kai supplies a less extreme example. After the Second Nightmare and his confrontation with the Ivory
Dragon, Chapter 748 says his **Awakened Aspect evolved to Ascended**. The text also calls such events
known but **incredibly rare**.

Therefore **Aspect Rank is not necessarily fixed forever**, but evolution should not be assumed to be
routine.

Sources:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-15.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-748-young-masters.html

### 2. Rank advancement reveals new Aspect Abilities

The underlying Aspect can remain the same while deeper powers appear.

Sunny remains **Shadow Slave**, while his status accumulates:

- Shadow Control
- Shadow Step
- Shadow Manifestation
- Shadow Incarnation
- Shadow Legion

Chapter 744 also makes the other important point: existing powers can **evolve/deepen**, not merely sit
unchanged next to a newly unlocked ability. Nephis's **[Longing]** similarly evolves after
Transcendence.

So the canon shape is not simply “one unrelated spell per rank.” It is better understood as deeper
expressions of one supernatural nature, with both new abilities and changes to old ones possible.

Sources:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-744-ascension.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-1592-humble-shopkeeper.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-1678-path-to-supremacy.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-2981-different-meaning.html

### 3. Aspect Legacy

Chapter 218 gives unusually clear rules:

- **every Aspect contains an Aspect Legacy**;
- very few Awakened ever receive/unlock one;
- each Legacy has a **unique set of requirements**;
- unlocking one can lead to powerful relic rewards;
- acquisition is rare enough that many Legacy Clans are historically founded around such a legacy.

Sunny's is **Shadow Dance**. Importantly, he had to understand and develop the dance/battle art himself;
the Spell recognized what he had achieved as his Aspect Legacy.

Later material broadens the concept further: not every Legacy is simply a conventional item/reward
track. Sunny's is a skillset with mastery stages, while Nephis's Legacy is tied to understanding and can
reward knowledge.

Source:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-218-shadow-dance.html

### 4. Legacy Relics

Early understanding says the reward is usually a Memory that synergizes with the Aspect, sometimes an
Echo. Sunny later proves the category can be much stranger: one of his Shadow Dance Legacy Relics is a
**fragment of Shadow's Domain**, not ordinary equipment.

Therefore “Legacy Relic = weapon/item” is too narrow.

### 5. Natural Awakening shows that Aspect realization and soul rank can be out of sync

Rain Awakens naturally **before** unsealing her Aspect. When she eventually does unseal it, she is
already an Awakened and its lower-rank expressions are available to her rather than forcing her back
through the Spell's ordinary timeline.

This is important canonically because it proves that “receive Aspect at rank X” is a property of the
Spell-assisted path, not a universal metaphysical requirement.

**Working relationship:**

```text
Aspect
├─ Aspect Rank (potential; can exceptionally evolve)
├─ Innate Ability (not universal/at least not always shown)
├─ Aspect Abilities / deeper expressions unlocked with advancement
└─ Aspect Legacy
   ├─ unique unlock requirement
   ├─ mastery/development
   └─ Legacy Relics / other rewards
```

That diagram is a research synthesis, not a literal in-novel diagram.

Notes:
- Do not assume every character whose Legacy is unmentioned lacks one; Chapter 218 says every Aspect
  contains one, but few people unlock it.
- Do not assume Sunny's unusually dramatic evolution is normal.

### Major staleness contradiction: Mordret

**CONFIDENCE: CANON for the late reveal; formal name still UNKNOWN**

Older Miraheze material still says Mordret's Flaw is **unknown**. That was true when the page was last
updated, but is no longer current lore.

Late-story material reveals that when twelve-year-old Mordret passed his First Nightmare, his Flaw
**shattered the original person into seven distinct, incomplete selves**. Different fragments inherited
different parts of the original. One fragment killed and absorbed five others; the surviving “other”
Mordret carried crucial qualities the dominant one lacked, including the shared death condition and
compassion-related parts of self.

By Chapter 2988 the final fragments are recombined and Mordret becomes whole again. The chapter then
makes the important distinction that being reassembled does **not** make him flawless: the damage,
memories, guilt, regret and shape imposed on his life remain like cracks in a repaired vessel. In other
words, this is a workaround/transformation of the condition, not evidence that a Flaw can simply be
deleted.

Current Fandom runes material still labels the **formal Flaw name as Unnamed**. A fan phrase such as
“Fractured Reflection” must therefore not be promoted into canon without a direct rune/status reveal.

### What produces a Flaw?

**CONFIDENCE: CANON for the general rule; INFERRED for the taxonomy below**

Chapters 2031–2032 are the clearest modern explanation:

- Flaws **do not originate from the Nightmare Spell**; the Spell helps Awakened discover them.
- They manifest the **law of imperfection**.
- Aspect and Flaw are **innately tied**.
- A Flaw is deeply connected to something fundamental about the individual.
- The relationship can involve identity, what the person cherishes, the nature of their power, or
  their fate.
- It is possible in principle to deduce someone's Flaw if you understand that person deeply enough.

Sources:
- https://www.webnovel.com/book/22196546206090805/84130766165960211 — Chapter 2031
- https://www.webnovel.com/book/shadow-slave_22196546206090805/these-three-words_84130776098064794 — Chapter 2032
- https://shadowslave.fandom.com/wiki/Flaw

#### Rain is the strongest causal case

Chapter 2029 says Sunny felt something fundamental shift in Rain **once she chose to spare Telle**.
Chapter 2030 identifies the change as attainment of her Flaw. Her eventual rune is **[Crown of Thorns]:
You cannot kill**.

This is very strong evidence that a profound personal choice can be involved in discovering/realizing a
Flaw whose nature fits that choice.

It still does **not** prove a universal rule such as “the last major choice in the trial becomes the
Flaw.” Canon does not give that algorithm.

Sources:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-2029-fortune-telling.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-2030-runs-in-the-family.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-2038-soul-of-a-poet.html

#### Working causal patterns — **INFERRED, not canon categories**

These are useful descriptive buckets for comparing examples, but the novel does not name them as a
system:

1. **Activation price:** Nephis suffers whenever she uses her Aspect.
2. **Same nature turned harmful:** Effie's enhanced physique brings enhanced needs; Morgan's cutting
   nature makes normal contact dangerous.
3. **Resource counterweight:** Samara spends essence spectacularly but regenerates it poorly.
4. **Existential survival pairing:** Jet's condition makes continued essence acquisition a requirement
   for existence, while her power provides a route to that essence.
5. **Personally cruel “benefit”:** Kai's lie detection is useful in the abstract and psychologically
   miserable for Kai specifically.
6. **Identity/fate fracture:** Mordret's Flaw literally fractures the person.
7. **Choice-aligned realization:** Rain's refusal to kill and later Crown of Thorns.

These examples show why “Flaw = random negative status effect” is not faithful to the text, but they do
**not** amount to a canonical Flaw-generation formula.

## A6. Can a Flaw be removed, cured, reversed or turned into an advantage?

**CONFIDENCE: CANON**

### Ordinary removal: no general method found

The modern explanation treats a Flaw as a fundamental part of a person's imperfection and growth, not
as a debuff pasted on by the Spell. Chapter 2032 describes Flaws as an insurmountable challenge that can
be **worked around** rather than simply conquered/removed.

### Worked around: yes

Sunny is the cleanest long-running example. **Clear Conscience** never stops existing, but he learns to
misdirect, omit, exploit phrasing and allow other people to reach false conclusions. Chapter 2033
explicitly reflects that he became a master of deception despite being unable to lie.

Rain also proves that the operation of a Flaw can depend on subjective belief. Chapter 2865 explains
that the universe does not physically stop a lethal result; Rain's own action is prevented while she
**believes** it would directly endanger/end a life. Asterion can therefore circumvent the restriction by
corrupting what she believes to be true. That is exploitation of the condition, not cure.

Sources:
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-2033-dire-flaw.html
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-2865-the-subject-of-murder.html

### A Flaw can be contextually advantageous

Kai's lie detection is an obvious practical advantage even though it is a genuine personal curse.
Morgan's Flaw becomes useful in Mirage City when supernatural powers are stripped away but Flaws remain;
her dangerous cutting contact still functions.

“Useful” therefore does not mean “not a Flaw.” The curse is personal/contextual, not a rule that every
mechanical effect must always reduce combat power.

### Reversal

**Weaver's Mask** is established to reverse Flaws rather than erase them. Reversal can create a new
condition that is at least as dangerous, so it should not be described as a cure.

**TODO:** add a fresh primary-source citation for the mask/reversed-Flaw examples before treating the
specific reversed forms as fully documented here.

### Mordret is exceptional, not a general cure precedent

By Chapter 2988 Mordret has reassembled the last pieces of himself, but the text explicitly rejects the
idea that this makes him free of his Flaw. The scars and consequences of being shattered still define
the terms of his existence.

So the best current conclusion is:

> **Flaws can be accommodated, exploited, circumvented or reversed, but no general method for simply
> deleting one's Flaw has been established.**

## A7. How do Aspect and Flaw relate for the same person?

**CONFIDENCE: CANON for intrinsic connection; INFERRED for patterns**

They are **intrinsically connected**, but not by one simple rule such as “the Flaw is always the direct
opposite of the Aspect.”

Chapter 2032 explicitly ties Aspect and Flaw together and suggests that finding one's Flaw may even be a
prerequisite for unsealing one's Aspect on the natural path. Flawlessness is also incompatible with the
path toward Transcendence.

Chapter 2033 supplies another important in-world rule of thumb: Cassie sees Rain's extremely severe,
broad Flaw and concludes that her eventual Aspect must be extraordinary — at least Supreme and likely
Sacred. This is an informed character judgement, not a numeric law, but it supports a relationship
between the magnitude of the Flaw and the potential of the Aspect.

Source:
- https://www.webnovel.com/book/shadow-slave_22196546206090805/these-three-words_84130776098064794
- https://novelfull.com/shadow-slave/chapter-2033-dire-flaw.html

### The relationship takes different forms

- **Nephis:** extraordinary power whose use directly imposes extraordinary suffering.
- **Effie:** the same physical nature is amplified in both beneficial capability and harmful need.
- **Samara:** the resource her Aspect spends is exactly the resource her Flaw regenerates poorly.
- **Jet:** her condition and her essence-harvesting powers form an existentially interlocked system.
- **Kai:** the Flaw is personally targeted and socially cruel rather than an obvious opposite of
  flight/voice/transformation abilities.
- **Mordret:** the Flaw attacks the integrity of the self, not simply one combat statistic.

### Sunny proves that the Aspect itself can have a dangerous downside separate from the Flaw

This distinction is essential.

Sunny's slavery condition is **not his Flaw**. Chapter 15 explicitly labels **[Shadow Bond]** an
**Innate Ability** of **[Shadow Slave]**. His actual Flaw is **[Clear Conscience]**.

Therefore a character can possess all three of the following at once:

```text
Aspect benefits / powers
Aspect-specific conditions or dangerous consequences
Flaw
```

not merely:

```text
Aspect = upside
Flaw = balancing downside
```

# Open questions

1. **Exact Aspect-generation mechanism.** Canon establishes affinities, choices, identity/fate and
   Nightmare-role influence, but gives no deterministic weighting or algorithm.
2. **Exact First-Nightmare role-selection mechanism.** Roles are not random and similarity of fate is
   strongly implicated; the governing rule remains unknown.
3. **How routinely a First Nightmare can change Aspect Rank.** Sunny proves dramatic evolution is
   possible; he is an exceptional case. Kai proves later Aspect evolution can also occur and is very
   rare.
4. **Most formal Aspect names.** Many powers and ranks are known while the rune-name remains unrevealed.
5. **Most formal Flaw names.** Effects are often known while the bracketed name is not.
6. **Quantitative Aspect–Flaw relationship.** Characters infer that a dire Flaw signals extraordinary
   Aspect potential, but no formula is given.
7. **Whether a profound choice creates a Flaw or reveals a Flaw that was already latent.** Rain tightly
   links choice and realization but does not resolve the metaphysics.
8. **Precise mechanics for several minor-character Flaws**, especially Kim, Tristan, Ki Song and Luster.
9. **Formal name of Mordret's Flaw.** Late-story mechanics are revealed, but current checked runes still
   label the formal name as unnamed.
10. **What exactly counts as “removing” a Flaw.** Mordret can become physically/psychologically whole
    again while the story insists the Flaw's scars remain; no general removal rule is known.

# Things I did not ask about that matter

1. **The Spell's runes are an interface/translation, not the underlying metaphysics.** Chapter 2029
   explains that Cassie's true power is insight; the Spell renders that insight as familiar runes. This
   matters whenever an English label is treated too literally.
2. **A First Nightmare can leave persistent traits from the person whose role the Aspirant occupied.**
   Sunny later identifies Temple Slave and two Attributes as coming from the nameless temple slave,
   while Fated was innately his.
3. **Natural Awakening separates soul rank from Aspect discovery.** Rain can be Awakened before having
   an unsealed Aspect, then reveal it later. The Spell-assisted sequence is therefore not a universal
   law of the power system.
4. **An Aspect may contain a catastrophic condition of its own.** Shadow Bond is the clearest proof that
   not every downside belongs under the Flaw heading.
5. **Aspect Rank and Soul Rank are distinct variables.** Sunny can have a Dormant soul core with a
   Divine Aspect; Kai's Aspect itself can evolve independently. This needs to be kept explicit when
   Section B is researched.
6. **The existing concise lore-reference note contains rank/progression statements that should be
   re-audited in Section B/D before promotion.** This file intentionally does not rewrite those sections
   early.
