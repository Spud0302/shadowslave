## Research safety

- Keep chapter references and concise paraphrases.
- Never commit chapter archives, scraped collections, transcripts, or substantial quotations.
- A community wiki is a research index, not decisive authority.
- Later explicit clarification outranks an early character assumption.
- Preserve UNKNOWN when evidence does not answer the question.
