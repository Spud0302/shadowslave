# Context packet — Lore research

## Goal

Answer one exact lore-sensitive question with chapter-backed evidence while separating canon from Minecraft design.

## Must read

- [[docs/LORE-SOURCE-POLICY]]
- [[docs/JAVA-LORE-ALIGNMENT]]
- [[brain/lore/index]]
- The relevant section under docs/lore-research
- Any current accepted decision that depends on the answer

## Workflow

1. State the exact mechanic, term, relationship, or chronology question.
2. Use community sources only to find candidate names and chapters.
3. Verify decisive claims against chapter text and check later clarification.
4. Check official publication wording or chapter identity where practical.
5. Record chapter numbers and concise paraphrases.
6. Classify every material claim as CANON, INFERRED, DESIGN, UNKNOWN, or COMPATIBILITY.
7. Record contradictions instead of choosing the convenient version.
8. Preserve flexibility when evidence remains ambiguous.

## Copyright boundary

Do not commit chapters, bulk text, transcripts, scraped caches, or substantial excerpts. Quote only the shortest wording necessary and identify its chapter.

## Output

Create a new immutable evidence note. If a project decision must change, create a separate proposed ADR rather than silently editing authority.
